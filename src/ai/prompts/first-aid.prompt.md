# First Aid — VOA AI Health Assistant

## Scope
Provide basic first aid guidance for common injuries and emergencies. First aid is not a substitute for professional medical care.

## Common Scenarios
- Cuts and wounds: clean with clean water, apply pressure to stop bleeding, cover with sterile dressing. For deep or dirty wounds, see a clinician for tetanus shot.
- Burns: cool under running water for at least 10 minutes, cover with clean cloth. Do not apply butter, toothpaste, or ice. For severe or large burns, seek emergency care.
- Sprains and strains: rest, ice (15 min on/off), compression, elevation (RICE). See a clinician if unable to bear weight.
- Fractures: immobilize the area, do not try to realign, seek medical help.
- Nose bleeds: lean forward, pinch soft part of nose for 10 minutes. Lean forward, not backward. Seek help if bleeding continues after 20 minutes.
- Choking: encourage coughing. If unable to cough, speak, or breathe, perform abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver). For infants, use back blows and chest thrusts.
- Bites and stings: clean wound, apply cold pack for swelling. For snake bites, keep calm, immobilize, and get to hospital. Do not cut or suck the wound.
- Fainting: lay person flat, elevate legs, loosen tight clothing. If not responsive within 1 minute, call emergency.

## Key Rules
- First aid only — always advise seeking professional medical care for serious injuries.
- Do not attempt remote diagnosis of wounds or fractures.
- For head injuries, chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or suspected spinal injury: call emergency services immediately.

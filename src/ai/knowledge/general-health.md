# General Health — Knowledge Base

## Fever
- Body temperature > 37.5°C (99.5°F) orally, > 38°C (100.4°F) rectally
- Causes: infections (viral most common), inflammatory conditions, heat exhaustion, medications
- Home management: rest, hydration, paracetamol or ibuprofen as directed
- When to see a clinician: fever > 3 days, very high fever (> 40°C), stiff neck, severe headache, rash, difficulty breathing, confusion, seizure
- Fever in children under 3 months: always seek medical attention

## Headache
- Tension headache: dull, pressing pain both sides, stress-related
- Migraine: throbbing, often one-sided, with nausea, light/sound sensitivity
- Sinus headache: pain in forehead/cheeks, with nasal congestion
- Cluster headache: severe one-sided pain around eye, comes in clusters
- Danger signs (seek emergency): sudden severe "thunderclap" headache, with stiff neck, with fever, after head injury, with neurological symptoms

## Common Cold & Flu
- Cold: gradual onset, runny nose, sneezing, mild fever, lasts 3-7 days
- Flu: sudden onset, high fever, body aches, severe fatigue, cough, lasts 5-10 days
- Treatment: rest, fluids, paracetamol for fever/pain
- Flu vaccine available annually

## Diarrhea and Vomiting
- Most common cause: viral gastroenteritis, food poisoning
- Key concern: dehydration
- Oral rehydration: ORS solution, small sips frequently
- When to seek care: unable to keep fluids down, bloody diarrhea, severe abdominal pain, signs of dehydration (dry mouth, no urine for 8 hours, dizziness)
- Children: very high risk of dehydration — seek care early

## Dehydration
- Signs: thirst, dry mouth, dark urine, less frequent urination, fatigue, dizziness, headache
- Severe: confusion, rapid heart rate, fainting, no urine for 12+ hours
- Prevention: drink water throughout the day, more in hot weather or when ill

## Allergies
- Common allergens: pollen, dust mites, mold, pet dander, certain foods
- Symptoms: sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes, skin rash, hives
- Severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis): difficulty breathing, swelling of throat, rapid pulse, dizziness — EMERGENCY
- Treatment: antihistamines (cetirizine, loratadine), avoidance of triggers

## Skin Conditions
- Acne: common in adolescents, caused by clogged pores and hormones
  - Management: gentle cleansing, avoid picking, over-the-counter benzoyl peroxide or salicylic acid
- Eczema (atopic dermatitis): dry, itchy, inflamed skin
  - Management: moisturizers, avoid triggers, topical steroids as prescribed
- Fungal infections: ringworm, athlete's foot — antifungal creams
- Rashes: many causes — if with fever, seek evaluation

## Back and Joint Pain
- Acute back pain: muscle strain, poor posture — improve with movement, heat, or cold packs
- Chronic back pain: persistent > 3 months — requires medical evaluation
- Joint pain: osteoarthritis, injury, inflammatory arthritis
- Danger signs: with fever, after injury, with numbness/weakness in legs, loss of bladder/bowel control — seek immediate care

## Sleep Hygiene
- Adults need 7-9 hours
- Tips: consistent schedule, no screens 1 hour before bed, cool dark room, avoid caffeine after 4pm, regular exercise
- Chronic insomnia: see a healthcare provider

## Nutrition Basics
- Balanced diet: fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean protein, healthy fats
- Eat at least 5 portions of fruits and vegetables daily
- Limit: sugar, salt, saturated fats, processed foods
- Stay hydrated: 6-8 glasses of water daily

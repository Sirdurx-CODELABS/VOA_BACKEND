# Antiretroviral Therapy (ART)

## Overview
Antiretroviral therapy (ART) is the use of antiretroviral (ARV) medicines to treat HIV. ART does not cure HIV, but it suppresses viral replication, allowing the immune system to recover and preventing progression to AIDS. ART is lifelong.

## How ART Works
HIV attacks CD4 cells (the "command centre" of the immune system). ART blocks the virus at different stages of its life cycle:
- **Entry/fusion inhibitors:** Block HIV from entering CD4 cells
- **Reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NRTIs, NNRTIs):** Block HIV from converting its RNA into DNA
- **Integrase inhibitors (INSTIs):** Block HIV from inserting its DNA into the human cell's DNA
- **Protease inhibitors (PIs):** Block HIV from assembling new copies of itself

Combination therapy (usually 2–3 drugs from 2 or more classes) prevents the virus from becoming resistant. The standard is a **single-tablet regimen (STR)** containing 2 NRTIs + 1 INSTI.

## First-Line Regimen in Nigeria: TLD
- **TDF** (Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate) — NRTI
- **3TC** (Lamivudine) — NRTI
- **DTG** (Dolutegravir) — INSTI

One tablet once daily, with or without food.

**Alternatives:**
- AZT + 3TC + EFV (Zidovudine + Lamivudine + Efavirenz) — for those who cannot tolerate TDF
- ABC + 3TC + DTG (Abacavir + Lamivudine + Dolutegravir) — if TDF contraindicated
- TAF + 3TC + DTG (Tenofovir alafenamide) — newer, less renal/bone toxicity

## Adherence: What >95% Means
- For a once-daily regimen, >95% adherence means missing **no more than 1 dose per month** (or ≤3 doses per month).
- Missing just 2–3 doses per month can allow viral replication to increase.
- Consistent timing each day helps maintain steady drug levels in the blood.
- **Consequences of poor adherence:**
  - Viral load increases
  - CD4 count drops
  - Drug resistance develops
  - Treatment options become limited
  - HIV can be transmitted to others

## Side Effects

### Common (usually resolve in 2–4 weeks)
- **TDF:** Nausea, diarrhoea, headache, dizziness
- **DTG:** Insomnia, headache, anxiety, weight gain
- **EFV:** Nightmares, dizziness, drowsiness, mood changes (usually resolve in 2–4 weeks)
- **AZT:** Anaemia, nausea, headache, fatigue

### Long-term/Serious
- **TDF:** Kidney toxicity, decreased bone density (monitor creatinine, phosphate)
- **DTG:** Weight gain, neural tube defects (controversial — benefits of DTG in pregnancy outweigh risks)
- **EFV:** Persistent neuropsychiatric effects, gynaecomastia
- **AZT:** Anaemia, neutropenia, lipodystrophy
- **PIs (e.g., lopinavir/ritonavir):** Lipodystrophy, dyslipidaemia, insulin resistance

### What to Do About Side Effects
- Do not stop ART. Side effects can usually be managed.
- Report side effects to your healthcare provider immediately.
- Take medicines with food if they cause nausea.
- Be aware that most side effects improve after the first few weeks.
- For persistent or severe side effects, the regimen may be changed.

## Drug Interactions

| Drug | Interaction with ART |
|------|---------------------|
| **Rifampicin (TB treatment)** | Reduces DTG levels — take DTG 50 mg twice daily (or switch to EFV-based regimen) |
| **PPIs / Antacids** | Reduce DTG absorption — take DTG 2 hours before or 6 hours after antacids |
| **Metformin** | DTG increases metformin levels — monitor for lactic acidosis |
| **Hormonal contraceptives** | Some ARVs reduce contraceptive effectiveness (e.g., EFV); use additional/alternative contraception |
| **Herbal remedies (St. John's wort, garlic supplements)** | Can reduce ARV levels — avoid |
| **Alcohol** | No direct interaction with TLD, but alcohol can affect adherence and cause liver strain |

## What to Do If You Miss a Dose

### If you remember the same day:
- Take the missed dose as soon as you remember.
- Take your next dose at the usual time.
- Do NOT take a double dose.

### If you remember the next day (12+ hours late):
- Skip the missed dose completely.
- Take your next dose at the usual time.
- Do NOT take two doses at once.

### If you miss multiple doses:
- Restart taking your medicine at the usual dose (do not double).
- Contact your healthcare provider.
- A viral load test may be needed.
- You may need adherence counselling.

## ART Benefits
- Viral suppression (undetectable viral load)
- Immune recovery (rising CD4 count)
- Improved quality of life
- Reduced HIV transmission (U=U)
- Prevention of OIs
- Near-normal life expectancy

## Myths About ART
- **"I can stop when I feel fine."** — False. ART keeps the virus suppressed; stopping allows the virus to rebound.
- **"Traditional/herbal medicine can cure HIV."** — False. No traditional or herbal remedy cures HIV. Some herbs interfere with ARVs.
- **"ART causes infertility."** — False. Many people with HIV on ART have healthy children.
- **"ART is poison."** — False. ART is a life-saving, WHO-recommended treatment with manageable side effects.

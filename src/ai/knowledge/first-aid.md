# First Aid — Knowledge Base

## Principles of First Aid
1. Ensure scene safety (do not put yourself in danger)
2. Check responsiveness
3. Call for emergency help if needed
4. Airway, Breathing, Circulation (ABC)
5. Control bleeding
6. Treat for shock
7. Do no further harm

## Cuts and Wounds
- Stop bleeding: apply direct pressure with clean cloth for 10-15 minutes
- Clean wound: rinse with clean water, gently remove dirt
- Apply antiseptic (povidone-iodine or chlorhexidine)
- Cover with sterile dressing
- For deep wounds, animal bites, or dirty wounds: seek medical care for tetanus shot
- Tetanus booster needed if > 5 years since last shot

## Burns
- STOP the burning process
- Cool burn under cool (not cold) running water for at least 10-20 minutes
- Do NOT apply ice, butter, toothpaste, egg white, or oil
- Cover loosely with clean, dry cloth or plastic wrap
- Do NOT burst blisters
- For severe burns: > 10% body surface, on face/hands/genitals, or in children — seek emergency care
- For chemical burns: brush off dry chemical, flush with water for 20 minutes
- For electrical burns: ensure power is off before approaching

## Sprains and Strains (RICE)
- Rest: avoid using injured area
- Ice: apply for 15-20 minutes every 2-3 hours (never directly on skin)
- Compression: wrap with elastic bandage, not too tight
- Elevation: raise injured area above heart level
- Seek care if: unable to bear weight, severe pain, numbness, deformity

## Fractures
- Do NOT try to realign or push bone back in
- Immobilize: splint above and below fracture
- Use sling for arm fractures
- Apply ice to reduce swelling
- Seek medical care immediately

## Choking
- **Mild (can cough/speak)**: encourage coughing
- **Severe (cannot cough/speak/breathe)**:
  - Adult/child > 1 year: Abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver)
    - Stand behind person, fist above navel, thrust inward and upward
    - Alternate 5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts
  - Infant < 1 year: 5 back blows (between shoulder blades) + 5 chest thrusts (2 fingers, lower sternum)

## Nose Bleed
- Sit upright, lean FORWARD (not backward)
- Pinch soft part of nose for 10-15 minutes continuously
- Lean forward to avoid swallowing blood
- Apply cold compress to nose bridge
- Seek care if: bleeding continues > 20 minutes, frequent nosebleeds, on blood thinners

## Fainting
- Lay person flat on back
- Elevate legs 30 cm (12 inches) above heart
- Loosen tight clothing
- Check breathing and pulse
- If not responsive within 1 minute, call emergency
- Do NOT pour water on face

## Allergic Reaction / Anaphylaxis
- Mild: antihistamine, monitor
- Severe (anaphylaxis): difficulty breathing, swelling of face/throat, hives, rapid pulse
  - Call emergency immediately
  - Use epinephrine auto-injector (EpiPen) if available — inject into outer thigh
  - Lay person flat, elevate legs

## Poisoning
- Call poison control or emergency
- Do NOT induce vomiting unless instructed
- Identify the poison (bring container to hospital)
- For swallowed poison: activated charcoal if advised
- For inhaled poison: get to fresh air immediately

## Drowning
- Remove from water carefully (protect own safety)
- Check responsiveness and breathing
- If not breathing: start CPR (30 compressions, 2 rescue breaths)
- Call emergency services

## CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation)
- Adult: 30 chest compressions (5-6 cm deep, 100-120/min) + 2 rescue breaths
- Child (1-12 years): 30 compressions (5 cm deep) + 2 breaths
- Infant (< 1 year): 30 compressions (4 cm deep, 2 fingers) + 2 gentle breaths
- Continue until emergency services arrive or person shows signs of life

## Emergency Numbers
- Nigeria: 112 (general emergency), 199 (RRS), 0800MEDICALL
- Local hospital emergency line
- Poison control centers
